=== COMPANY OVERVIEW ===
Nexariza AI is an artificial intelligence consulting and engineering company, founded in 2024 by Ahmad Yasin. Headquartered in Lahore, Pakistan, Nexariza AI operates as a global remote-first team serving clients in 15+ countries. The company maintains a 98% client satisfaction rate with an average rating of 5.0/5 based on published reviews.

Nexariza AI serves clients across various industries including healthcare, retail, travel, agriculture, automotive, and finance, delivering AI solutions tailored to each industry's specific needs.

=== INTERNSHIP REQUIREMENTS & ELIGIBILITY ===
Nexariza AI offers competitive internship programs for students and early-career enthusiasts. Below are the standard requirements for applying:

1. Technical Skills & Domains:
- AI/ML Interns: Basic understanding of Python, machine learning concepts, libraries (Scikit-learn, TensorFlow), and foundational knowledge of RAG or LLMs.
- Data Science Interns: Proficiency in Python, data preprocessing, feature engineering, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), and data visualization.
- Full Stack Development Interns: Familiarity with modern web development frameworks (such as React, Next.js, or Django), version control (Git/GitHub), and basic API integration.

2. General Criteria:
- Educational Background: Current students or recent graduates pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, or related fields.
- Commitment & Schedule: Ability to commit to the 8 to 12 weeks program duration with a flexible remote schedule.
- Passion & Problem Solving: Strong analytical mindset, eagerness to learn production-grade AI systems, and ability to collaborate in a remote team environment.

3. How to Apply for Internships:
Candidates can check active openings and submit their applications directly through nexariza.com/jobs or reach out via email for queries.
